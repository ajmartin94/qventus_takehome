# Add-on, Urgent and Emergent Cases

*Northwind Regional Medical Center, Perioperative Services. Policy PS-112. Simulated.*

## Urgent and emergent cases

Urgent and emergent requests are not booked through the elective scheduling queue. The requester is connected with the anesthesiologist in charge and the OR desk, who assign the urgency class and the room. The scheduling office passes on the patient, surgeon, procedure, side, the urgency as stated, the requested timing and a contact.

## Add-on cases

The schedule for a surgery date closes at 14:00 on the business day before. Requests received after the schedule closes are add-ons and go to the OR desk.

## Bumping a scheduled case

Only the anesthesiologist in charge may displace a scheduled case to make room for an urgent or emergent one. The scheduling office may list the cases that would be affected. The surgeon taking the room informs the surgeon whose case is displaced.
