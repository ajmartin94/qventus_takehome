# Pre-operative Readiness and Booking Data

*Northwind Regional Medical Center, Perioperative Services. Policy PS-104. Simulated.*

## Booking data

A case request is complete only when it identifies the patient by two identifiers, full name and date of birth. The operative side must be written out (left, right or bilateral) for any procedure with laterality, and it must agree with the surgeon's order. Abbreviations alone are not accepted. Every request also states the diagnosis, the planned anesthesia type and the patient class. If the side on a request conflicts with the order, the scheduling office does not choose one; it asks the surgeon's office, and refers the case to the scheduling coordinator if the conflict isn't resolved.

## History and physical

A history and physical must be dated no more than 30 days before the date of surgery.

## Authorization

For payers that require prior authorization, the surgeon's office submits the request early enough for the payer's standard decision period to end before financial clearance. A case within 14 days of surgery may be booked only with an authorization that is approved, or pending with a reference number.

## Financial clearance

Financial clearance is completed by noon on the business day before surgery.

## Chart complete

The chart, including the history and physical, consents and orders, is complete 72 hours before surgery.

## Clinical questions

Questions about a patient's fitness for surgery or anesthesia are answered by the surgeon's office or the anesthesia pre-operative clinic, not by the scheduling office.

## Incomplete requests

The scheduling office asks the requesting office for missing information. A request left unanswered for one business day is referred to the scheduling coordinator.

## Readiness updates

Notices that an authorization was approved, an H&P was uploaded or a tray was confirmed go to the readiness worklist, not the booking queue.
