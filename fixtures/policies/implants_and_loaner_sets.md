# Implants and Loaner Sets

*Northwind Regional Medical Center, Sterile Processing and Perioperative Services. Policy SPD-210. Simulated.*

## At booking

Any case that uses an implant is booked with the implant system, the vendor, whether a loaner set is needed and whether a vendor representative will attend.

## Loaner sets

Loaner instrument sets must arrive in Sterile Processing 48 hours before the scheduled start of surgery. A set that is new to the hospital must arrive 3 business days before, to allow for inventory, inspection and a trial reprocessing. Sets arriving late may delay or cancel the case.

## Vendor representatives

When a vendor representative attends, the vendor confirms the representative for the case date. Representatives check in through the vendor credentialing system on the day of surgery.
