# Block Time and Release

*Northwind Regional Medical Center, Perioperative Executive Committee. Policy PEC-301. Simulated.*

## Block time

Block time is prime time (07:30 to 15:30) allocated to a surgeon or service group. Only the block owner may book into a block until it is released.

## Automatic release

Unbooked time in a block is released to the open pool automatically: 14 days before the surgery date for robotic blocks, and 7 days before for all other blocks, at 07:00. Released time may be booked by any credentialed surgeon.

## Early release

An owner may release block time early by notifying the scheduling office. Released time is not returned to the owner.

## Asking an owner to release time

A surgeon who wants time in another owner's unreleased block may ask that owner to release it. The scheduling coordinator sends the request; the owner decides.
