# Rooms and Shared Equipment

*Northwind Regional Medical Center, Perioperative Services. Policy PS-120. Simulated.*

## Robotic rooms

Robotic cases are performed in OR-5 and OR-6, the robot-ready rooms. Robots are shared between those rooms: a robotic case needs both a robot-ready room and a free robot of the right type (orthopedic or soft-tissue). Robotic blocks follow the 14-day release in policy PEC-301.

## Turnover

Turnover is planned at 30 minutes in the main operating rooms and the robot-ready rooms, 45 minutes in the cardiac room and 15 minutes in the minor procedure room.

## Cases the scheduling office books by hand

Some cases need more than a room. Cardiac cases with a planned ICU admission are booked together with an ICU bed through bed management. Cataract lists that run in paired rooms are built by the scheduling coordinator with the ophthalmology group. These requests are referred to the scheduling coordinator.
